Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains what data OS collects, why, and your choices. OS is operated by Sosa Technologies, which is the data controller. We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable law.

Data we collect

  • Account data: email, password (stored securely), name or display name, age, state, and the profile details you add.
  • Photos, statuses and voice notes you upload.
  • Verification media: a short liveness video and comparison photo, used only to confirm you are real and reviewed privately by authorized moderators.
  • Messages and call data: chats you send, and call details needed to connect voice and video calls.
  • Location: approximate or precise location, only when you use Nearby, to show distance to others.
  • Device data: push notification token and basic technical information to deliver notifications and keep the app working.
  • Safety data: trusted contacts you add to the Date safety kit, and reports or blocks.

Device permissions

OS may ask for camera and microphone (verification, calls, voice notes), location (Nearby), photos (profile pictures) and notifications. Fingerprint login is handled by your phone; we never receive your biometric data. You can change permissions in your phone settings.

How we use data

  • Create your account and show your profile to other members according to your settings.
  • Verify identity and keep OS free of fake profiles.
  • Deliver chats, calls, requests and notifications.
  • Review reports, enforce our Terms and protect members.
  • Maintain, secure and improve the Service.

We do not sell your personal data.

Who we share it with

  • Other members: your profile, and your photo only as your privacy settings and photo requests allow.
  • Service providers that run OS for us, such as cloud hosting, push notifications (Firebase) and call infrastructure (LiveKit), bound to protect your data.
  • Authorities, where the law requires or to prevent serious harm.

Retention

We keep data while your account is active. When you delete your account we remove your profile, photos, statuses, messages and verification media, except what we must keep to meet legal duties or resolve abuse, which is then kept only as long as needed.

Your rights

You may access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict some processing, and withdraw consent. You can use the app settings or email . You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Children

OS is not for anyone under 18. If we learn a person under 18 has an account, we remove it.

Security

We use encryption in transit and access controls to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.
