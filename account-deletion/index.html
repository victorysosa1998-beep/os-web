OS DATING · DELETE YOUR OS ACCOUNT

Delete your account

You can delete your OS account and data at any time.

In the app

  • Open OS and go to You then Settings.
  • Tap Delete account.
  • Enter your password and confirm.

Request deletion without the app

You do not need to reinstall or sign in to the app to make this request. Email us from the address registered to your OS account with the subject Delete my OS account. Please include your OS display name or another account identifier so we can locate the account. Do not send your password, verification video, or financial information. We may contact you to verify ownership before deletion.

Email an account-deletion request

We will verify the request and process it within a reasonable period. If we must retain limited information for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons, we will handle it as described in the Privacy Policy.

What is deleted

  • Profile, photos, statuses and voice notes
  • Messages and connections
  • Verification video and comparison photo
  • Safety-kit contacts and preferences
Deletion is permanent. We may keep limited records where the law requires or to prevent abuse, only as long as necessary. See our Privacy Policy.
