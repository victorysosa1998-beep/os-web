Safety Tips

Meet and chat safely.

Keep it on the app

Stay in OS chat until you trust someone. Never share financial details, passwords or codes, and never send money to someone you have just met.

Meet safely

Meet in a public place, tell a friend where you’re going and arrange your own transport. Use the Date safety kit to alert trusted contacts.

Trust your instincts

If something feels off, block and report. Moderators review every report.
