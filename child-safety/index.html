OS DATING · CHILD SAFETY STANDARDS

Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 22 October 2026

OS is a dating service intended only for adults aged 18 and over. Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexualization of minors, and any attempt to use OS to contact or exploit a child are strictly prohibited.

Age restriction

OS is not designed for or permitted to be used by anyone under 18. Users must provide accurate age information. If we become aware that an account belongs to a person under 18, we may restrict or remove the account and take appropriate safety steps.

Prohibited conduct and content

  • Any sexual content involving a minor, including CSAM.
  • Grooming, sexual exploitation, trafficking, coercion, or attempts to arrange sexual contact with a minor.
  • Soliciting, creating, sharing, possessing, or distributing CSAM.
  • Threats, blackmail, or attempts to obtain sexual images or personal information from a minor.
  • Using a fake age or identity to bypass the adult-only restriction.

Reporting and feedback

Users can report profiles, messages, or safety concerns using available in-app reporting tools. You can also report suspected child-safety concerns directly to our Child Safety Contact at sosatechnologies.support@gmail.com. Please include the relevant OS username/profile identifier and a description of the concern. Do not email or redistribute illegal images or videos.

Review and enforcement

We assess child-safety reports and take appropriate action based on the circumstances, which may include removing content, restricting or terminating accounts, preserving relevant information where lawful and necessary, and cooperating with competent authorities. We will report suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation to the appropriate authorities or reporting channels where required by applicable law or platform policy.

Compliance and contact

Sosa Technologies aims to comply with applicable child-safety laws and Google Play’s Child Safety Standards policy. Our designated Child Safety Contact is the OS support team, reachable at sosatechnologies.support@gmail.com. This contact can receive reports about CSAE, CSAM, grooming, and other child-safety issues.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or law enforcement. Do not use OS support as an emergency response service.
