Data practices

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This page gives a plain-language overview of categories of information that may be processed when you use OS Dating. The exact data handled depends on the features you use, app version and service configuration. The Privacy Policy explains purposes, sharing, retention and your choices.

Google Play Data safety: this website page is not a substitute for the Data safety form in Google Play Console. The developer must declare data collection and sharing based on the released app, its backend and every included SDK.

Information categories

Account and profile

Information you provide to create an account and build a profile, such as contact details, display name, age and profile fields.

Photos, posts and audio

Images, status updates, voice notes and other content you choose to upload or send, where those features are available.

Messages and calls

Messages and technical information needed to deliver chat or calls. The exact content and metadata processed depends on the communication feature and service provider.

Location

Location information may be used if you choose a location-based feature such as nearby discovery. Review the permission prompt and device settings before enabling it.

Verification and safety

Information submitted for verification, reports, blocks and safety features, where enabled. Such information should be accessed only for legitimate verification, support, safety or legal purposes.

Device and diagnostics

Technical information such as app/device identifiers, notification tokens, crash reports or diagnostics may be processed by the app or its configured service providers.

Why information is used

  • To create and operate accounts and core app features.
  • To show profiles and deliver messages, calls and notifications.
  • To support verification, security, abuse prevention and user reports.
  • To maintain reliability, troubleshoot issues and meet legal obligations.

Service providers and sharing

OS may rely on infrastructure and service providers to operate features. The actual providers and data shared with them must be verified against the production configuration and are described in the Privacy Policy. We do not list a provider here as a confirmed processor unless it is actually used in the released app.

Your choices and deletion

You can review app permissions in your device settings. For privacy questions or requests, email sosatechnologies.support@gmail.com. To request account deletion without using the app, visit Delete your OS account.
